 # Not So Fun Halloween

  A top-down 2D rogue-like built in Unity, where survival depends on how well you hold your ground when the sun goes down. Play as Edmund, an angry pumpkin who went mad when he saw his family turned into jack-o'-lanterns. Now he hates Halloween and seeks revenge on trick-or-treaters.

  ## About

  *Not So Fun Halloween* is a Halloween-themed rogue-like set across a repeating day/night cycle. Days are for exploring and
  preparing; nights bring waves of enemies that test your defenses and force you to adapt on the fly.

  ## Features

  - **Day/Night Cycle** — Alternating phases that shift pacing and threat level, from calm preparation to high-pressure
  survival
  - **Melee & Ranged Combat** — Multiple ways to fight back, each suited to different enemy types and situations
  - **Upgrade System** — Grow stronger between runs/waves with upgrades that shape your playstyle
  - **Diverse Enemy Types** — Different enemies with distinct behaviors keep encounters varied
  - **Base Defense** — Fortify and defend your base as nights grow harder

  ## Built With

  - Unity
  - C#
